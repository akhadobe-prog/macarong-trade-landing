About Us

Our Services

Chemical Storage

We store all kinds of chemical products, being aware that each part must work perfectly and must fit in with the rest to achieve excellence.

Packaging Liquid and Solid Chemicals

Our facilities are engineered to handle complex liquid formulations. We possess specialized equipment required for safe mixing and packaging of highly reactive and sensitive substances, including acids and polymers.

Solid-state chemistry capabilities include pre-crushing, lump breaking, screening, sieving, mixing, impregnation, encapsulation and production of tablets and pellets.

Handling

We handle all kinds of chemical products, taking great care of what we have in our hands and locating products according to product standards.

Transportation

We transport dangerous goods according to ADR, IMDG, ICAO-TI and RID regulations, optimizing time, quality and cost.
