About Us

We provide services you need for managing, storage, packaging, handling and transport of your chemical products.

Our Services

Chemical Storage

We store all kinds of chemical products, being aware that each part must work perfectly and must fit in with the rest to achieve excellence.

Packaging Liquid and Solid Chemicals

Our facilities are engineered to handle complex liquid formulations. We possess specialized equipment required for safe mixing and packaging of highly reactive and sensitive substances, including acids, polymers and other chemical products.

Our capabilities span the entire production cycle: pre-crushing and lump breaking to advanced screening, sieving, mixing, impregnation, encapsulation, and production of tablets and pellets.

Handling

We handle all kinds of chemical products, taking great care of what we have in our hands, locating the product in accordance with product standards.

Transportation

We transport dangerous goods according to ADR, IMDG, ICAO-TI and RID regulations, providing solutions adapted to each product and optimizing time, quality and cost.

Specialized Solutions

Maximize Capacity with Flexitanks

Reduce shipping costs and increase volume. Flexitanks allow 30–40% more product than traditional drums by utilizing the full capacity of 20ft containers. We use recyclable liners to minimize environmental impact.

Secure Hazardous Cargo with ISO Tanks

ISO tank services provide a robust solution for transporting aggressive chemicals. Designed for road, rail and sea transport, ISO tanks ensure safe and efficient multimodal logistics.

Guaranteed Food Safety

Food-grade transport solutions for oils and juices. Equipment is cleaned and inspected according to strict EFTCO standards with quality control procedures.
